What Drives Us

Our Mission, Vision & Purpose

Our Mission

To democratize world-class technical education and make high-quality, career-transforming learning accessible to every motivated individual on the planet — regardless of where they live or how much they earn.

Our Vision

A world where anyone with the will to learn can acquire in-demand skills, build meaningful careers, and contribute to shaping the future of technology — without financial or geographical barriers.

Our Purpose

We exist to close the global skills gap. Every course we publish, every mentor we recruit, and every community we build is driven by one purpose: empowering people to change their lives through learning.

Our Promise

We promise to always put learner outcomes first. Every decision we make — from hiring instructors to building features — is measured against one question: does this help students succeed faster?

TechLearn HQ — Gurugram, India

How We Started

From a Laptop in a Shared Flat to a Global Learning Platform

In 2019, our founders, Bashir Hussain Ebrahimi and Zubaida Alizada, were working as senior engineers at a startup in Pune. During their time there, they noticed a recurring problem: many talented and highly motivated individuals were failing job interviews—not because they lacked intelligence, skills, or determination, but because they did not have access to the right learning resources. This realization inspired them to create TechLearn, with the mission of making high-quality education accessible to everyone

"We didn't build TechLearn to make money. We built it because we'd seen what happens when the right education reaches the right person at the right time. It changes everything."
— Bashir Hussain Ebrahimi, Co-Founder & CEO

Today TechLearn operates from offices in Gurugram, Bangalore, and London, employs 60+ full-time team members, and has helped students in 50+ countries build careers in tech.

Our Journey

How We've Grown Over the Years

2019 — Q1
TechLearn is Born

Bashir Hussain Ebrahimi record the first 12 courses from a Pune apartment. The platform launches with 50 beta users and a dream.

2019 — Q4
First 1,000 Students

Organic word-of-mouth drives enrollment past 1,000. First revenue milestone hit. The team grows to 5 people including two dedicated instructors.

2020 — Q2
Seed Funding & Expansion

Raised ₹2.5 Crore seed round from AngelList India investors. Moved to a proper office in Gurugram. Course catalog expands to 30 courses.

2021 — Q1
5,000 Students & Series A

Crossed 5,000 enrolled students. Secured $1.2M Series A led by Blume Ventures. Launched mobile apps on iOS and Android.

2022 — Q3
Global Launch & London Office

Opened London office, launched multilingual subtitles (EN, HI, ES, FR, DE). Students now in 35+ countries. Team grows to 40 employees.

2023 — Q2
AI-Powered Learning Features

Launched AI learning assistant, personalized course recommendations, and adaptive quizzes. Courses surpass 80. Instructor network grows to 40+.

2024 — Present
10,000 Students & Beyond

Celebrating 10,000+ students, 100+ courses, and 8,500+ certificates issued. Planning Series B and expansion into Southeast Asia and Africa.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

These aren't posters on a wall. They're the principles every TechLearn team member is hired for and held to.

Radical Accessibility

Great education belongs to everyone. We price fairly, offer free tiers, provide scholarships, and build features that work on low-bandwidth connections.

Outcomes Over Vanity

We measure success by student job placements, salary increases, and career pivots — not monthly active users or vanity metrics. Outcomes first, always.

Relentless Curiosity

We're learners ourselves. The tech landscape changes daily. We stay at the cutting edge so our students are always learning what the industry actually needs.

Community First

Learning is social. We build tools and spaces for students to support each other, collaborate, celebrate wins, and find mentors — because no one succeeds alone.

Integrity in Everything

We never oversell. Course descriptions are honest, ratings are unfiltered, and our refund policy is zero-friction. We earn trust by deserving it.

Continuous Improvement

Every course is updated. Every feature is iterated. Every complaint is a gift. We ship fast, learn faster, and never declare anything "done."
